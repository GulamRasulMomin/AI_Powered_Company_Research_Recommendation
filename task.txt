Task : Build an AI-Powered Research & Recommendation Agent

Create a system that takes the name of a company as input and generates a structured intelligence report.

Example Input : 
    ● Adani Realty
    ● Sobha
    ● Prestige Group
    ● Brigade Group
    ● Puravankara
    ● Any other company

The System Should Generate : 

    1. Company Overview

        Provide a concise summary of:
            ● What the company does
            ● Industry
            ● Scale
            ● Geographic presence

    2. Key Business Information

        Identify:
            ● Major offerings
            ● Recent developments
            ● Expansion plans
            ● Important public information

    3. Potential Business Challenges

        Based on your research and reasoning, identify:
            ● Possible challenges
            ● Operational bottlenecks
            ● Sales challenges
            ● Customer experience challenges

        Explain your reasoning.

    4. AI Opportunities

        Suggest where AI can help the company.

        Examples:
            ● Automation
            ● Customer engagement
            ● Sales
            ● Operations
            ● Analytics
            ● Document processing

        Do not give generic answers.
        Provide company-specific suggestions.

    5. Personalized Pitch

        Assume you are meeting the CEO of the company.

        Create a one-page personalized pitch explaining:
            ● Why you reached out
            ● What opportunities you identified
            ● What AI solutions you would recommend
